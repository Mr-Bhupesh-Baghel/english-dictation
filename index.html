How to use English Dictation

  1. Choose a English paragraph
  2. Copy the text and paste it into the box.
  3. Click Start Dictation, listen to each word, and type what you hear.
  4. Press Check (or the Space bar) to check your answer. Use Hear Again whenever you need it.
